# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1, summarized here in the terms that matter for a repository about checking
claims.

## The standard

Everyone taking part in this project, in any of its spaces, is expected to make
participation free of harassment for everyone else, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Behavior that keeps the project worth contributing to:

* Criticize work rather than people, and say what would change your mind.
* Accept a review that shows you were wrong; give one that says why.
* Assume the other person had a reason, and ask for it before assuming the worst.

Behavior that is not acceptable:

* Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
* Insults, personal or political attacks, and trolling.
* Harassment, in public or in private.
* Publishing someone else's private information without their explicit permission.

## Scope

This applies in every space this project uses, including issues, pull requests,
discussions, and commit messages, and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers, through a direct message on
GitHub or the contact method listed on the repository owner's GitHub profile. Do not use
a public issue: a report should not require the reporter to publish the incident.

Every report is reviewed, kept as confidential as the review allows, and answered. A
maintainer involved in an incident does not decide its outcome.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may respond in proportion to what happened: a private correction, a warning,
a temporary ban from the project's spaces, or a permanent ban. Comments, commits, issues,
and other contributions that violate this document may be edited or removed, with a
reason given.
