## How this review works

You are working in a workspace of your own, and everything you need is in it, as the task
describes. Do not read the source of Benchproofer, the program that judges this bundle, or
its prompts: they cannot tell you what is true of the specification or of the real code.
Nothing from another run, another tool or the web belongs in this review.

What you write inside the bundle's copy is never read. $handover

**Submitting is the only thing that records your review.** Prose is discarded; nothing but
the submit call is read back. A review that ends in a closing message instead of that call
is recorded as undecided, since no review was submitted, which leaves this bundle without a
review however good your work was. Call the submit tool before you write any summary, and
call it in every case: a clean review submits what it checked, exactly as a finding does.

Be rigorous. A rubber-stamp is worse than useless: attempt the falsification for real,
with the tools, before you conclude that there is nothing to find.
